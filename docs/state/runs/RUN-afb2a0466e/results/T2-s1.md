## Findings, ranked

**F1 (High): the edge device is outside any evidenced residency boundary (HC-01, SEC-001).**
- The proposal says edge location and custody after departure are UNRESOLVED. A vessel-borne edge (E2) would carry the first copy of raw data out of the jurisdiction.
- F-16 is only a proposed inventory row. The edge is not in F-01 to F-15 as far as I can tell from the proposal. I did not read the matrix to confirm.
- E1 (a fixed port-side edge) is only proposed.
- If E2 were selected, HC-01 would be Violated: raw data would be stored outside the originating jurisdiction with no recorded legal basis. Reject E2 unless D-009 and D-011 are answered.

**F2 (High): the "no figures or compliance claims" assertion is not evidenced (HC-12).**
- The proposal itself says no EV record supports its HC-12 statement or its FACT labels. HC-12 is therefore Unresolved, not Satisfied.
- The proposal's own "Conditional on review" label fits.

**F3 (High): several shared components remain, so HC-02 is not met in either option.**
- In B1, cell-level identity, key management root, logs, telemetry, management plane, anchor log and verifier are all SHARED. B1 has at least five candidate HC-02 violations. Credentials and compute context are listed as shared, and HC-02 names both.
- B2 still leaves these UNRESOLVED: the management plane (SHARED), identity issuance (D-017), the log collector, the key root of trust (D-013) and the physical substrate (D-010).
- The Options text says B2 is "not eliminated by any hard constraint". That is true only because the unresolved items are not yet decided. A shared management plane that can reach all tenants would meet the HC-02 "reject when" test of a shared credential or compute context. The wording should not suggest B2 is cleared.
- The "operator-owned evidence common to a delivery" item is unresolved (D-015, D-016). It could be a shared store across shipowners.
- I also could not check whether the edge device is shared across shipowners.

**F4 (High): tamper detection is not always achieved (SEC-008, HC-08).**
- The proposal names two undetected cases as UNRESOLVED. One is edge compromise before first upload. The other is an administrator rewriting the store and chain inside the anchor window, which needs receipts or other copies to detect.
- It also names tail or whole-delivery deletion before the first anchor or receipt.
- SEC-008 requires that modification or deletion is always detected. On this proposal's own account, HC-08 would be Violated if the selected design were shown to allow undetectable modification or deletion. That is not yet shown, because the design is still open.
- The tamper-test rows are all "untested".

**F5 (Medium): the verifier's independence rests on an unverified assumption (SEC-008, HC-08).**
- The proposal assumes an independent in-jurisdiction operator and anchor exist at each port. It marks both unverified.
- If the verifier is operated by the store administrators, independence is not met, and the proposal says so.
- If the verifier is also a single shared instance, it is a candidate HC-02 violation.

**F6 (Medium): key custody is undefined (SEC-001, HC-01, HC-02).**
- SEC-001's acceptance criterion covers key management.
- The proposal gives no jurisdiction or custody design for keys. The root of trust depends on D-013, and the edge signing keys depend on D-019.
- I found nothing on where backup copies of keys sit.
- A key held or escrowed outside the jurisdiction, or one root shared across shipowners, would be a violation. Neither is shown.

**F7 (Medium): support and administration access is not shown to be denied from outside (SEC-002).**
- The design says administration and break-glass are inside the cell. It treats the operator model (D-033) and in-jurisdiction staff availability as open.
- Support access, the management plane (F-08) and break-glass on the edge are not shown to be denied by configuration or by negative tests. None exists yet.
- Until then, SEC-002 stays Conditional.

**F8 (Medium): outward readers are all unapproved flows (HC-01).**
- This covers shipowners, certifiers, registries and arbitrators. The proposal treats them as cross-border flows needing a verified legal basis (D-009, D-011).
- It correctly proposes that nothing is delivered outward until the flow is approved.
- Hub staff and AI inference (Z3, Z4) are described as prohibited. I did not verify that from the matrix.
- Backups, disaster recovery (F-02, F-03), replicas, logs and telemetry are asserted in-jurisdiction. No configuration or negative-test evidence exists.

**F9 (Low): disposal and anchoring leave open points for counsel.**
- Disposal appends a record and keeps the payload hash. Whether keeping the hash is permitted is an ASSUMPTION that counsel must confirm.
- External anchoring is treated as "No" until D-009 or D-011 allow it. That is a correct posture.

**F10 (Low): document hygiene.**
- A stray empty file, `EVIDENCE-STORE-PROPOSAL.md.new`, is reported by the author. I did not check whether it exists.
- Shape item 4 is duplicated in the text.
- The proposal says it set aside the relative-links convention. A reviewer should decide which rule applies.

## Recommendation

Do not accept the proposal. Do not accept B1 as written: it carries at least five candidate HC-02 violations.

The vessel-borne edge (E2) should be rejected unless D-009 and D-011 permit the flow. The fixed port-side edge (E1) should be pursued only as a pending proposal.

Before any approval, a human must settle these items:
1. D-009 (legal meaning of "within the jurisdiction"), D-010 (hosting model) and D-011 (approved cross-border flows).
2. The edge device's location and custody, plus key custody and root of trust (D-013, D-019).
3. The verifier operator, a split or justified management plane, and the retention and isolation decisions (D-015, D-016).
4. The two tamper cases (edge compromise and the anchor window), either closed or explicitly accepted by a human.

Suggested cheapest tests:
- A negative test showing that cross-jurisdiction access and a cross-tenant query are both denied.
- A reorder, tail-delete and whole-delivery-delete drill against a test store.
- A disconnection reconciliation test per FR-006.

Then have qa-engineer or design-reviewer record EV evidence for the HC-12 statement and check the cited facts against the three files I did not read.
