# Risk Register

Status: v0.1, 2026-10-28. Architecture risks and their mitigations. Entries are qualitative on purpose. **No probability, loss amount or schedule impact is stated, because none has evidence behind it.** Likelihood and impact use Low, Medium, High or Unassessed; Unassessed is the honest value when nobody has assessed it.

How it is used: a perspective verdict of Concern in a review record must link a risk here. A risk linked to a Must requirement stays Open until its mitigation is in a design that has passed the gate. The gate checks that linked risk IDs exist; it does not judge whether a mitigation is good enough, which is the reviewer's job.

Status values: Open, Mitigated, Accepted (owner has accepted it knowingly), Closed.
