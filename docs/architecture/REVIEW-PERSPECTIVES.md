# Review Perspectives

Status: v0.1, 2026-10-28. Every major design is evaluated through all ten perspectives. The reviewer records one verdict per perspective in the review record. A perspective that was not assessed blocks approval; a perspective with verdict Fail rejects the design (see [DESIGN-REVIEW-GATE](DESIGN-REVIEW-GATE.md)).

Verdicts:
- **Pass**: the evidence listed in the "Must see" column exists and shows no rejection trigger.
- **Concern**: a weakness the reviewer can describe, with a linked risk in the [RISK-REGISTER](RISK-REGISTER.md). Does not reject; approval still needs the risk to be tracked.
- **Fail**: a rejection trigger is present. The design is rejected until changed.
- **Not assessed**: the reviewer could not judge it, usually because the evidence does not exist yet. This is a valid, honest verdict and blocks approval. It is never rounded up to Pass.

Questions for each perspective are in the [DEFENSE-QUESTION-BANK](DEFENSE-QUESTION-BANK.md). Hard constraints are in [HARD-CONSTRAINTS](HARD-CONSTRAINTS.md).
