# 06 Threat model

Status: proposed, Phase 0. A design-time threat model for the target architecture, written before any code. It names threats and the controls the design relies on; it does not claim any control exists or works. **Production-agent security tests are separate from the coding-harness tests** and are all pending (see the [evaluation report](../evaluation/EVALUATION-REPORT.md), suites `harness` and `production`). STRIDE letters: S spoofing, T tampering, R repudiation, I information disclosure, D denial of service, E elevation of privilege.

## Assets

| Asset | Data classes | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Raw measurements and evidence packages | DC-01 to DC-04 | The basis of every claim; residency-bound (HC-01) |
| Calculation records, methodology and factor sets | DC-05, DC-06 | Reproducibility; a wrong factor propagates into certificates (HC-05) |
| Certificates, approvals, filings | DC-08, DC-09 | Tamper evidence, the quality manager's authority (HC-03, HC-04) |
| Customer data and disclosure policies | DC-11, DC-12 | Isolation per shipowner (HC-02) |
| Signing keys and secrets | DC-16 | Compromise forges certificates |
| Agent operational records and memory | DC-13 | A route for poisoning and leakage |
| Platform configuration, source, pipelines | DC-17 | A route to everything else |

## Attacker classes

Malicious or careless insider (operator, support, engineer); compromised edge device; external attacker on the internet; malicious feedstock supplier or document author; a shipowner trying to see another's data; a compromised agent or prompt-injected model; a compromised dependency or build; a cloud or vendor administrator acting outside the jurisdiction; a legal request in another jurisdiction.

## Highest residual risks

1. **Authenticity at the source (TM-01, TM-04).** Everything downstream is only as trustworthy as the device identity and signing at the meter or logger. If meters cannot sign and the edge is not physically controlled, the best the platform can do is corroborate and flag; it must not present unauthenticated readings as trusted. The meter landscape (D-019) is the single most important unknown for evidence integrity.
2. **Legal reach and global services (TM-07, TM-08, TM-24).** Technical controls cannot settle what a provider, a support engineer or a foreign authority may do. These need counsel (D-009) and a deliberate decision on the identity provider's scope and the support model (D-033).
3. **Key custody (TM-10).** An undecided mechanism (D-013) is the biggest open design decision for HC-03 and HC-08.
4. **The human approver (TM-23).** The platform can bind an approval to a hash; it cannot make the quality manager read it.
5. **Unbuilt tests.** Every production-agent threat above has a pending test. Nothing in this model has been demonstrated against a running system.
